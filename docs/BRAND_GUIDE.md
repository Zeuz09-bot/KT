# Keraunous Tech Store — Brand Guide & Design System

## 1. Design Philosophy: Quiet Tech Luxury
Keraunous Tech Store embodies **Quiet Tech Luxury** for the Nigerian market. The aesthetic prioritizes generous negative space, understated confidence, architectural harmony, and high tactile quality over bright neon colors or aggressive sales stickers.

Every touchpoint — from physical unboxing and shopping bags to the digital showcase website — reflects boutique restraint, precision craftsmanship, and authentic provenance.

## 3. Typography System

### 1. Editorial Headline (Launch / Prestige Posts)
- **Primary Typeface**: `Cinzel` (`next/font/google`), with serif fallbacks.
- **Weights**: Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600).
- **Styling**: All-caps with wide letter-spacing (`tracking: +150 to +200` / `tracking-editorial`).
- **Application**: Exclusively for high-end campaign headers like *Coming Soon*, *Excellence & Authenticity*, or prestige curated banner eyebrows.

### 2. Brandmark & Standard Headlines
- **Primary Typeface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`next/font/google`), geometric sans-serif.
- **Weights**: Bold (700) to ExtraBold (800) for titles; Medium (500) & SemiBold (600) for navigation and subtitles.
- **Styling**: Clean, geometric, and modern. Keep headlines sentence-cased or uppercase with moderate tracking (`tracking: +25` / `tracking-display`).
- **Application**: Logo mark, section headings, product card titles, navigation menus.

### 3. Body & Technical Specs
- **Primary Typeface**: `Inter` (`next/font/google`).
- **Weights**: Regular (400) & Medium (500).
- **Styling**: Neutral, legible, unadorned.
- **Application**: Ensures gadget specifications, integer Naira (₦) prices, delivery policies, and customer notices remain razor-sharp and functional.

## 4. Core Composition & Layout Rules

### The 60–30–10 Composition Rule
- **60% Neutral Canvas**: Limestone Cream (`#F3EFEA`) or clean Pure White space (`#FFFFFF`). Sets a calm, inviting, unhurried backdrop.
- **30% Grounding Tones**: Slate Navy (`#3B4854`) or Obsidian Charcoal (`#18191B`). Used for structural navigation, packaging cards, and typography.
- **10% Refined Accents**: Brushed Silver (`#D4D8DD`) or Champagne Foil (`#C5B49C`). Used for metallic badges, fine hairline borders, and subtle prestige indicators.

### Generous Breathing Room
- Maintain at least **30% to 40%** of every graphic layout and product viewport completely clear of text or clutter to preserve the boutique luxury feel.
- Avoid aggressive stickers, giant discount blasts, or fake urgency countdowns.

## 5. Physical Materials, Packaging & Art Direction

### Packaging & Unboxing
- **Boxes**: Deep black or dark slate matte soft-touch finish with anti-fingerprint lamination.
- **Printing Technique**: Blind debossing paired with silver or champagne hot-foil stamping.
- **Carry Bags**: Heavy uncoated paper (250+ gsm) with dyed-to-match grosgrain or satin ribbon handles.
- **Tissue & Inserts**: Pure white tissue paper with subtle charcoal logo watermark pattern.

### Photography & Art Direction
- **Lighting**: Warm, low-angle natural daylight with soft, sharp architectural shadows.
- **Surfaces**: Honed travertine, microcement pedestals, smooth matte plaster walls.
- **Prop Styling**: Sparse product arrangements (maximum 2–3 hero items per shot) paired with soft organic elements like fluid silk or natural stone.
